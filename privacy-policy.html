Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Greenk ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and discloses information when you download and play Night Bubble Shooter: Starry Pop and our mobile games and applications.

1. Information We Collect

Our games are designed to be played without requiring personal account creation. However, our third-party service providers may collect non-personally identifiable information automatically:

  • Device Information: Device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers (e.g., Google Advertising ID).
  • Game Analytics: Gameplay progress, level scores, and session duration.

2. Third-Party Services & Advertising

We use third-party advertising and monetization partners, primarily Google AdMob and Google Play Services. These services may collect and use pseudonymous advertising identifiers to serve relevant ads (including Banner, Interstitial, and Rewarded Video ads) in accordance with Google's privacy policies.

You can learn more about how Google uses data by visiting: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

3. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such data, please contact us immediately so we can remove it.

4. Opt-Out & Advertising Preferences

Players can reset or opt out of personalized advertising at any time through their Android device settings: Settings > Google > Ads > Delete advertising ID or Opt out of Ads Personalization.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us via our official support channel: greenk.support@gmail.com.
