Privacy Policy

Application: Night Bubble Shooter Developer: Greenk Package ID: com.skgamers2105.bubbleblitz Effective Date: November 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Greenk ("we", "our", or "us") manages information when you download, install, and play Night Bubble Shooter (the "Game") on Android devices through the Google Play Store.

Key Summary: We respect your privacy. You do not need to register an account or provide personal information (such as your real name, email, address, or phone number) to play our game. Your game progress is stored securely on your own device. We integrate trusted Google services (Google AdMob) for monetization and game performance.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

We believe in data minimization. The Game does not require registration or personal logins. Therefore, we do not collect or store:

  • Your real name, postal address, or physical location.
  • Your personal email address, phone number, or contacts list.
  • Your credit card, banking, or financial account details.
  • Biometric, health, microphone, or camera data.

2. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

To provide a smooth, offline-friendly experience, the Game saves your gameplay state directly in your device's private local storage (HTML5 LocalStorage / IndexedDB). This includes:

  • Current level progression (Levels 1 to 200).
  • In-game star ratings, high scores, and achievement timestamps.
  • Virtual coin balance and power-up inventory (Bombs, Rainbows, Fireballs).
  • User settings (sound effects volume, background music toggle, haptic vibration preferences).

This data stays on your personal device and is not uploaded to our own private servers.

3. Information Collected Automatically by Third Parties

To support ongoing maintenance, optimize gameplay stability, and deliver in-game advertising, we integrate certified third-party software development kits (SDKs):

Provider Purpose Data Handled Privacy Documentation
Google AdMob Monetization & In-Game Ads (Banner, Interstitial, Rewarded Video) Google Advertising ID (AAID), IP Address (for general geolocation), Ad interaction telemetry, Device model, OS version Google Ads Privacy
Google Play Services Core App Runtime & Platform Security Device identifiers, app installation status, system crash diagnostics Google Play Privacy

4. Device Permissions Used by the Application

The Game requests only the minimal system permissions necessary for its intended features:

  • android.permission.INTERNET: Required to communicate with Google AdMob servers for loading banner, interstitial, and rewarded video ads.
  • android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Allows the app to detect internet availability so ad requests can be handled smoothly without freezing gameplay.
  • android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Allows the app to send optional reminders (e.g., daily reward chest ready, energy refills). Players can grant, deny, or revoke this permission at any time in Android System Settings.
  • android.permission.VIBRATE: Provides tactile haptic feedback when bursting bubbles, aiming trick shots, or conquering boss levels. Can be disabled within the in-game Settings menu.

5. In-Game Advertisements & Player Choices

Our game is offered free of charge, supported by advertising. We adhere to Google Play's Better Ads Standards:

  • Banner Ads: Displayed safely at the bottom of the screen, away from all interactive controls to eliminate accidental clicks.
  • Interstitial Ads: Displayed only at natural gameplay transition points (such as level victory or defeat), with reasonable frequency caps.
  • Rewarded Video Ads: 100% voluntary and player-initiated. Players choose whether to watch an ad in exchange for in-game rewards (e.g., extra coins or booster items).

Opting Out of Personalized Ads:

You can reset or opt out of personalized interest-based advertising at any time through your Android device settings:

Open Android Settings > Google > Ads > Tap "Delete advertising ID" or toggle "Opt out of Ads Personalization".

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA & Google Play Families Policy)

Night Bubble Shooter is designed for a general audience (ages 13 and above). We do not knowingly solicit or collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has provided personal information to us, please contact us at greenk.support@gmail.com, and we will promptly take appropriate action to remove such information.

7. Data Retention & Deletion Policy

In accordance with Google Play Data Safety requirements, players have complete control over their data:

  • Local Game Data Deletion: You can permanently erase all local game progress, coins, and settings at any time by either clearing the app's storage in Android Settings > Apps > Night Bubble Shooter > Storage > Clear Data, or by uninstalling the Game.
  • Advertising Data Deletion: You can reset or delete your Google Advertising ID through your Android device settings as described in Section 5.
  • Inquiries & Requests: For any questions or deletion inquiries regarding diagnostic records, please reach out directly to our support team at greenk.support@gmail.com.

8. Global Privacy Rights (GDPR & CCPA/CPRA)

Depending on your geographic location, you may have specific statutory privacy rights:

  • European Economic Area & UK (GDPR): You have the right to request access to, rectification of, or erasure of any pseudonymous personal data processed by our third-party partners, and to object to or restrict processing.
  • California Residents (CCPA/CPRA): We do not sell personal information for monetary consideration. Third-party ad partners may process identifiers for targeted advertising. You can opt out via your device's Google Ads settings.
